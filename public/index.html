APAAR VerifyStudent data and verification for AICTE-approved institutions
Prototype with sample data. Names, IDs and institutions are made up, and nothing here connects to DigiLocker or the real APAAR registry. Changes stay in this browser.
What this prototype simulates
  • The APAAR registry and DigiLocker identity lookup are a short sample list inside the page.
  • The one-time code stands in for DigiLocker's consent step. A real build would use its consent API.
  • The fingerprint is a SHA-256 hash of the attested record. A real build would also sign it with the institution's digital signature certificate and keep it on a server.
  • Access to a record is limited to what the student approved, and each access is written to the audit log.
